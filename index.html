About me

Sport operations found me on a race route in Leicester, waving marathon runners past a roundabout at seven in the morning — and I've been chasing that same adrenaline ever since.

Between an MSc in Sport Management, a placement consulting on a club's growth strategy, and enough matchdays to know exactly how a hundred-person crowd moves, I've built the kind of operational instinct that doesn't come from a textbook.

I'm looking for a Sport Operations or Event Management role where that instinct — and the spreadsheets behind it — actually gets put to work.

How I work

No two events run the same, but three things hold true wherever I'm working — whether that's a national hockey final or a taekwondo scorecard table.

01

Field-of-play focus

I've stood pitch-side at hockey finals and internationals with my radio in hand, and there's nothing quite like knowing exactly where you need to be the second the pressure hits.

02

Calm under logistics pressure

There's a particular kind of satisfaction in watching a hundred-plus crowd flow through a car park without a single bottleneck — that's usually the moment I know the day's going well.

03

People-first service

Whether I was chatting with fans at Leicestershire CCC or coaching someone through a session at FITZONE, I've always cared more about remembering their name than ticking a box.
